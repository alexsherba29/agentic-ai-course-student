# Support ticket and escalation policy

This policy explains when the support assistant should open a ticket and what the ticket must contain.

Open a support ticket only when:

1. The problem is not explained by a known outage on the service status page, and
2. The self-help steps in the relevant guide did not fix the problem, or no guide covers it.

Do not open a ticket for a known outage: one ticket per outage is enough, and the IT team opens it.

Every ticket must contain:

1. A short title that names the service, for example "VPN: certificate error after restart".
2. What the user tried, and the exact error message if there is one.
3. The time of the last attempt.

Never put passwords, one-time codes or other secrets in a ticket. Never ask a user for their password, and never tell anyone to turn off a firewall or other security software.

Priority: use "high" only when many users are affected or someone cannot work at all; otherwise use "normal".

A support assistant must get approval from a human support agent before it creates a ticket.
